Commercial Kitchen Cleaning in South Florida

Commercial kitchens are the hardest-working — and most heavily inspected — rooms in any food business. Airborne grease settles on ceilings, hoods, and exhaust systems, creating fire risk, foul odors, and the exact conditions health inspectors flag. Left unchecked, that buildup can slow service, fail an inspection, or shut you down.

Ceilings R Us keeps restaurant, grocery, hospital, and institutional kitchens clean from the ceiling down. We degrease acoustic tiles and open ceilings, clean exhaust hoods and vents, wipe down stainless steel and walls, and set up recurring maintenance so your kitchen stays inspection-ready year-round across Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties.
